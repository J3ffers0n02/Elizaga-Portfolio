Nature of Tasks

DOST-FPRDI had two main needs. First, they wanted a way to view and filter their past trainings by date and location to help with reporting and planning. A system was built to organize and display this data more clearly. Second, their current process for issuing seminar and webinar certificates involved manually checking if attendees completed all required forms. To make this process more efficient, an automated system was developed. These projects help improve how they manage and present their training activities.
